Who We Serve

Dancers Without a Partner

You Don’t Need a Partner to Dance! At Adam Hanna Ballroom Dance, you’ll be paired with an experienced, championship-level instructor who will guide you every step of the way. Whether you want to dance socially, perform, or compete, you’ll always have a skilled partner by your side.

Dancers Looking to Upgrade Their Skills

Ready to Level Up Your Dancing? Join our community of passionate dancers who are always striving for more. With personalized coaching and proven championship expertise, we’ll help you refine your technique, master new styles, and reach your next big goal-whether it’s a showcase, a competition, or simply dancing with more confidence and flair.

New Dancers

Curious About Ballroom? Start Here! If you’re new to ballroom dance, you’re in the perfect place. Our welcoming, supportive environment makes it easy and fun to get started. Dip your toes in with a beginner class or private lesson, and discover how quickly you can fall in love with the music, movement, and community.
